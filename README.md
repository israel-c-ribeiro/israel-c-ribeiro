## ◆ About

Postdoctoral researcher at the **University of Mons**, where I combine density functional theory,
machine-learning interatomic potentials and data science to understand and design halide perovskites
and other energy materials — atom by atom.

Ph.D. from **USP** on the surface passivation of 2D hybrid perovskite thin films, an **MBA in Data
Science & Analytics**, and three years in industrial analytical labs before that. Founder of Quantum
Master Inc., a small scientific-consulting venture.

**Looking for** a research or R&D role where simulation, machine learning and materials chemistry meet.

`Mons, Belgium` &nbsp; `PT · EN · FR` &nbsp; `Open to research & R&D roles`

---

## ◆ What I work on

| | |
|---|---|
| **First-principles simulation** | DFT of surfaces, interfaces, defects and thin films — from bulk halide perovskites to 2D Ruddlesden–Popper phases. |
| **ML potentials & structure search** | Neural-network interatomic potentials coupled with structural search on hybrid organic–inorganic interfaces; MD trajectories at scale on HPC. |
| **Materials informatics** | Clustering, dimensionality reduction and descriptor engineering to shrink the experimental search space before anything is synthesised. |
| **Theory ↔ experiment** | A hands-on sol-gel synthesis and characterisation background that turns simulation output into synthesis targets partners can act on. |
